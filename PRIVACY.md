# Privacy Policy

HLS Player does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data, browsing history, stream URLs, playback information, telemetry, or analytics.

The extension accesses HTTP and HTTPS requests only to identify top-level HLS (`.m3u8`) navigations and to load the stream selected by the user for local playback.

No data is sent to the developer or to any service operated by the developer.
